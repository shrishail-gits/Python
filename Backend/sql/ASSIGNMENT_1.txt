ASSIGNMENT_1
USING SPECIAL OPERATORS.
------------------------

1. Display all orders placed during the year 2024 whose status is either
   Delivered or Pending.

2. Display all orders placed by employees except Employee ID 2
   whose status is not Delivered.

3. Display all menu items that cost between ₹150 and ₹300
   and belong to Restaurant IDs 1, 5, or 6.

4. Display all menu items that do not cost between ₹150 and ₹300
   and do not belong to Restaurant IDs 2 and 4.

5. Display all restaurants located in Mumbai, Delhi, or Jaipur
   whose restaurant names do not match "Royal Thali".

6. Display all restaurants that are not located in Delhi or Rajasthan
   and whose address is on a road.

7. Display all menu items served by Restaurant IDs 2, 3, or 6
   except Fish Curry.

8. Display all orders that were not placed during the year 2023
   and whose status is neither Cancelled nor Pending.

9. Display all restaurants whose city name includes the letter 'A'
   and are not located in Tamil Nadu.

10. Display all menu items costing from ₹100 to ₹250
    whose description is not related to potatoes.

11. Display all orders placed between
    01-Nov-2024 and 31-Dec-2024
    by Employees 2 or 8
    whose status is Delivered or Dispatched.

12. Display all menu items except those costing ₹100, ₹150, or ₹350
    whose item name is Masala Dosa or Dal Baati.

13. Display all restaurants except Restaurant IDs 2 to 5
    whose city name does not include the letter 'I'.

14. Display all orders placed from October 2023 to December 2024
    whose status is not Delivered.

15. Display all menu items priced between ₹200 and ₹300
    whose description is related to spices or traditional food.

16. Display all restaurants whose phone number is available
    and whose address is on a road
    except those in Delhi.

17. Display all menu items whose description is available,
    whose name is either Masala Dosa, Dal Baati, or Ghevar,
    and whose price is outside the range ₹100 to ₹200.

18. Display all orders whose status is Delivered or Accepted,
    placed by employees other than Employee ID 8,
    and not placed during the year 2023.

19. Display all restaurants except those located in Kolkata
    whose restaurant name includes the letter 'T'
    and whose state is not Maharashtra.

20. Display all menu items belonging to restaurants other than
    Restaurant IDs 1 and 5,
    whose description is not related to fish,
    and whose price is between ₹120 and ₹350.
